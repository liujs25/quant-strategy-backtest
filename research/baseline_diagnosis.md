# Baseline diagnosis (inspected 2017–2018, exploratory)

The baseline is preserved in `audit/baseline_verified_20261124.zip`; no commit or push was made.

1. **Signal strength:** all four fitted models lose to both simple baselines in RMSE. Daily Rank IC is ARIMA 0.0163, forest -0.0072, LSTM -0.0007, Transformer 0.0136. These weak rankings are consistent with low economic signal-to-noise. `baseline_prediction_deciles.csv` tests whether high predicted ranks earn higher realized returns; it is descriptive and uses the already-inspected period.
2. **Feature quality:** eight short-horizon OHLCV ratios omit longer momentum, relative cross-sectional features, liquidity, market context and longer volatility scales. This motivates bounded multi-scale additions; it does not establish that the new features predict returns. Feature ICs on new validation data are retained separately.
3. **Fit behavior:** both neural networks select epoch 1; train error falls but validation error rises over six epochs. This is evidence of overfitting under the original small-signal MSE target, not an argument to add capacity unconditionally. New neural models use layer normalization, AdamW decay, dropout, lower learning rate and rank targets; larger models may still fail and all outcomes will be retained.
4. **Objective mismatch:** minimizing pooled MSE can mostly estimate a near-zero mean while portfolios depend on cross-sectional ordering. LambdaRank/pairwise objectives and centered rank targets directly test the ordering hypothesis. Ranking scores are not return estimates; their raw numerical RMSE/R² has no economic interpretation.
5. **Portfolio construction:** the 30-bp threshold often holds cash and creates sparse daily round trips. Forecast errors around a hard threshold can amplify turnover. The new search tests 1/5/20-session holding/rebalance schedules and keeping incumbents until they leave the top 40 ranks. It also compares 95%-invested equal weight with inverse volatility sizing.
6. **Costs:** ARIMA earns 5.38% gross annualized but -0.27% after 15 bp per leg. Forest/LSTM/Transformer already have negative gross annualized returns (-1.60%/-1.74%/-0.09%). Thus costs explain ARIMA's loss, but do not explain away the other models' failure. Two-sided annualized turnover is 36.75/8.80/27.23/14.90 respectively. Turnover reduction cannot create predictive information; it must be validated net of the same cost assumption.
7. **Research integrity:** 2017–2018 is exploratory henceforth. New model/portfolio selection uses only 2021–2023 validation with purged chronological training. 2024–2025 is not downloaded until a frozen selection record exists. Future missing securities must be reported and cannot trigger performance-based substitution.
